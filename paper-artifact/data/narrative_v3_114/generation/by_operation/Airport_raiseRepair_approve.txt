An approval decision records the supplied decision and suggestion in a new ApprovalHistory associated with the selected Repair and returns that history. Both the Repair and the deciding Staff member must exist. When reject is true, a role-1 member advances Process from 0 to 1, role 2 advances it from 1 to 2, and role 3 advances it from 2 to 3. When reject is false, the repair instead enters the rejected stage, Process 5.
