Renewing a loan requires an existing User, BookCopy and matching Loan, with the user in NORMAL status. The copy must not be reserved, its loan due date must be after today and the Loan must have no overdue fee. A Student may have fewer than 3 renewals and a non-Student user fewer than 6. The renewal count increases and the renewal date becomes today. Starting from the previous due date, the extension is 20 days for a BACHELOR Student, 40 for a MASTER Student, 60 for another Student and 60 for a non-Student user. Renewal returns true.
