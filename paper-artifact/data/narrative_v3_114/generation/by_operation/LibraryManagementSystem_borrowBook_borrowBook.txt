A borrowing request identifies a User and a BookCopy by user identifier and copy barcode. Both must exist, and the user must have NORMAL borrowing status with no suspension days. A BACHELOR Student may have fewer than 20 loans, a MASTER Student fewer than 40, another Student fewer than 60, and a Faculty user fewer than 60. The copy must be AVAILABLE, or be ONHOLDSHELF, marked reserved and have an open reservation matching this user.

Borrowing creates a new unreturned Loan linked to the user and copy, dated today, and adds it to both the user's loan collection and the copy's loan history. Its due date is 30 days for a Student and 60 days for a Faculty user. The user loan count increases by one, the copy becomes LOANED, and the new loan's 3-, 10-, 17- and 31-day overdue flags are false. If the copy was ONHOLDSHELF, its reserved flag is cleared and the matching reservation is closed. The operation returns true.
